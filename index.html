About NCL

Built by Players,
For Players

Next Cricket League (NCL) was born from a simple belief that Karachi's raw cricketing talent deserves a professional stage.

Founded in the streets of Karachi, NCL organizes hard ball cricket tournaments across the city, giving local players a platform to compete, shine, and get noticed. From T20 battles to 40-over tournaments, we bring structure, passion, and professionalism to grassroots cricket.

Our mission is to discover and develop the next generation of Pakistani cricketers, right here in Karachi.
